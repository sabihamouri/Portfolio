Business Model & Web Platform

RentAll BD

RentAll BD is a peer-to-peer rental platform designed for people who need items such as cameras, tools and eventwear occasionally but do not want to purchase them. The platform connects people who need items temporarily with owners who can lend their unused items. The project combines business model development with a web-based platform concept and was developed as a team project.

Full-Stack Development

ধার দে

ধার দে is a Bengali-language web application designed to help people track small informal debts between friends. Instead of relying on memory or notebooks, users can keep track of who owes whom and manage their debts more easily. The project focuses on making debt tracking simple, social, and easy to use. It was developed as a team project using full-stack web development.
